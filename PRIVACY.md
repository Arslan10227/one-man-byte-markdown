# Privacy Policy for OneManByte - Markdown Viewer & Editor

**Last updated:** August 28, 2026

**OneManByte** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use **OneManByte - Markdown Viewer & Editor** (the "Application").

---

## 1. Summary

- **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or analytics.
- **Offline & Local-First:** All file viewing, editing, parsing, rendering, and exporting take place 100% locally on your device.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** The Application contains no third-party trackers, advertisements, or profiling SDKs.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **Personal Data:** We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, IP address, device identifiers, or contact details.
- **Document Content:** The contents of the Markdown files, diagrams, formulas, and text documents you open, create, or edit in the Application never leave your device.
- **Usage & Diagnostic Telemetry:** We do not collect crash reports, keystrokes, click tracking, or analytics.

---

## 3. Local File System & Permissions

The Application requires standard local file system access solely for the core functionality you initiate:
- Opening markdown and text files from your local storage.
- Saving modified documents to your local storage.
- Reading and writing user preferences (e.g. selected theme, font size, file association settings) stored locally on your device via standard local storage.

No files or settings are ever uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

---

## 4. Internet Connectivity & External Services

- The Application operates completely **offline**.
- No network connections are initiated by the Application during normal operation.
- Math rendering (KaTeX), diagram processing (Mermaid), and syntax highlighting (highlight.js) are bundled natively within the application package and execute entirely client-side without external CDN calls.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for users of all ages.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this repository with an updated revision date.
